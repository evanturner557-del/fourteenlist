Questions

Common questions

Is it on sale now?

Not yet. Fourteenlist is a draft product. Ask to hear when it's ready.

Does it make me compliant?

It helps you organise information. Your council's environmental health team and the Food Standards Agency are the authority on what you must do.

Does it cover Natasha's Law labelling?

No. That applies to prepacked-for-direct-sale food and needs full labels.

Fourteenlist is an organising template. Check the Food Standards Agency's guidance for your legal duties.
